# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (Existing codebase)

## Users

Couples or friends in pairs who want to help each other make progress together.

## Product Purpose

To facilitate mutual growth and habit building for pairs (couples/friends) through shared goals, check-ins, and wishes. Success means users consistently engage with each other and achieve their shared goals.

## Positioning

A progress and habit-tracking tool specifically designed for pairs, focusing on mutual support and shared milestones rather than solitary tracking.

## Operating Context

Mobile-first web environment (used as an app). Users will likely check in daily, view their partner's progress, and interact with shared goals and wishes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Current capabilities include goal setting, daily check-ins, partner viewing, and wishes.
Constraint: Must be responsive and mobile-friendly as it is intended to be developed into an app.

## Brand Commitments

Visual style constraint: Cartoonish, pixel art style (as explicitly requested by the user).
